Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains the intended operation of HOWNOMALY Oracle, an owner-operated video research and publishing assistant. The Google/YouTube integration is currently being configured. No Google account has been connected to this instance yet.

1. What information may be accessed

Once the owner explicitly signs in and approves the requested scope, HOWNOMALY Oracle may access basic YouTube channel data available under the youtube.readonly permission, such as channel identity, title and channel-associated metadata needed to confirm the correct channel. Google authorization tokens may be processed to maintain this owner-approved connection. This informational website itself has no login form and does not request Google passwords or OAuth tokens.

2. How it is used

Data is used to verify that the integration belongs to the correct HOWNOMALY channel, monitor authorized channel information and support the owner's private video-production workflow. The initial read-only permission cannot upload, modify or delete videos. If additional permissions are introduced later, they will require a separate consent and purpose review.

3. Storage and protection

The planned application runs on an owner-controlled Oracle Cloud server. OAuth credentials, when configured, are intended to be stored in access-restricted server files rather than on this public website or in its source code. Limited technical records and channel metadata may be kept for troubleshooting and operation. Because authorization has not yet been completed, no claim is made here that a live Google token is already stored.

4. Sharing and third parties

HOWNOMALY does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Service providers involved in operation may include Google/YouTube, Oracle Cloud for the backend, and GitHub Pages for this public information site. This static information site includes no advertising or analytics scripts.

5. Retention and deletion

Access to Google data is retained only as long as needed for the owner-operated connection or until access is revoked. The owner may revoke access at Google Account connections. For deletion of server-side authorized data, contact the project owner through the support address listed on the Google OAuth consent screen. Please do not post passwords, tokens or personal data publicly.

6. Google API data policy

HOWNOMALY Oracle's use of data received from Google APIs is intended to comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

7. Changes and contact

This page will be updated if the application's scope or data-handling practices change. For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the support contact listed on the application's Google authorization screen.
